Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What this app is

The Tiny Why Studios uploader is a private tool used only by Tiny Why Studios to publish its own animated short films to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What data it uses

The app uses the YouTube Data API and the Instagram Graph API with the channel owner's own authorisation in order to:

  • upload our own videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to our own channel and account;
  • read basic details of our own channel to confirm the connection works;
  • read view and engagement counts for our own posts, to learn which topics our audience enjoys.

It does not collect, store or share any personal data about viewers or any other users.

How credentials are stored

The access tokens the app needs are stored privately: on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in a private repository. They are never published, sold or shared with third parties.

Google user data

Tiny Why Studios' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features, this app relies on the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions and from Instagram's Apps and websites settings.
